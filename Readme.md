# Quest Cards for Frosthaven & Crimson Scales

Have you ever sat down with your friends and wondered:

* "Which scenarios do we have available right now?"
* "Why did we want to go there again?"
* "Did we already complete this scenario (and forgot to tick it off)?"

The quest cards are intended as a remedy for all these.

## Usage

* Print out the quest cards
* Prepare three bags (or other suitable containers) for the cards and label them:
  * Locked
  * Available
  * Completed
* At the beginning of a campaign, all quest cards start in "locked" 
* Once a scenario is unlocked, move the corresponding quest card "locked" to "available"
* When you want to play a scenario, you can take your pick from all the quest cards in "available"
* Once a scenario has been completed, move its quest card to "completed"
